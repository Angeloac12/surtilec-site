# Organic Growth Workflow

## Goal

Attract industrial purchasing and maintenance teams in Colombia and turn
technical searches into a WhatsApp conversation or a structured quote request.

## Events

The child theme emits privacy-conscious events to `dataLayer`, GA4/Site Kit when
`gtag` is available, and Microsoft Clarity when its API is loaded:

- `product_search`
- `product_search_result_click`
- `quote_start`
- `quote_form_start`
- `quote_submit`
- `quote_validation_error`
- `bom_upload_start`
- `quote_add_product`
- `whatsapp_click`

Events include page type, path, traffic channel and product SKU when available.
They never include names, email addresses, phone numbers or form contents.

## Content rhythm

Publish one source-backed technical article per week after Surtilec review. Each
article should answer one commercial or technical question, show the direct
answer first, include the official source, link to a real product category and
end with a quote CTA.

The current topic pool is:

- THHN versus THWN-2.
- VFD cable and shielding.
- Control versus instrumentation cable.
- Tray cable TC/TC-ER.
- AWG selection and voltage drop.
- RETIE and NTC 2050 requirements.
- How to submit a multi-line material list.

The homepage now surfaces the three latest published resources. Resource
articles link into mapped product categories, and mapped category pages link
back to the relevant guides. The mapping uses existing URLs only.

## Weekly review

1. Review Search Console queries and internal searches with no result.
2. Select one query with clear purchase or maintenance intent.
3. Validate technical facts against an official manufacturer or regulator source.
4. Publish the answer, category links and quote CTA.
5. Review WhatsApp clicks, quote starts, submissions and response time.

Do not publish an unverified product, unsupported technical claim, copied
distributor text or third-party image without written permission.
